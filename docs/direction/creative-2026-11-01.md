## 1. Summary

1. The product's promise ("public, verified trading records") is not legible on the first screen: signed out at
   360 px, the feed never says "verified", "broker" or "record" above the fold; the definition sits in the footer.
2. The careful voice already exists in the connect, consent and /how screens. The direction extends it everywhere.
3. Three Must or policy gaps outrank every style question: TR-5 ("Disconnected ‹date›") is not rendered; demo
   stock images and pages print "verified"; stock pages and the stock image headline a security's own returns (F3b).
4. The disclaimer is exact everywhere, but not in plain view: footer-only on long pages, and 22 px on three
   1200-wide images (about 7 CSS px in a phone preview).
5. One name per thing: "Demo" (never "Sample"), "average per trade", "likely between", one join verb
   ("Publish your record"), "Verified" defined once and linked.
6. Visual direction "Ledger Ink": ink is the identity, colour is spent only on meaning (blue-green gain, vermilion
   loss, plum demo, amber attention, blue focus). Verified is ink, never the gain colour.
7. Gain and loss always differ by sign (true minus), mark (▲/▼), words and position against the 0% line; colour
   is the third channel. A flat state exists.
8. Motion never comments on outcome: no count-up, no celebration, no interpolated chart frames, reduced motion
   honoured globally.
9. The share card stays inside the owner's F-4 decisions. The built card lags F-4 in four places (chart series,
   trust-line table, period end, preview text); these are build gaps, not proposals.
10. Seven owner questions are collected in one packet (§7); none is changed here.

## 4. Pass 2: directions A to H

**A. "On the Record" (brand strategist).** Positions the product as the checkable alternative to an
unverifiable image of a gain: "Trading records imported read-only from the broker. Losses included." with the
tagline "The record, not the screenshot." Three pillars (sourced, not stated; the record shown whole, losses
included; uncertainty said plainly), a register of "calm, exact, even-handed", and the rule that badges describe
states of the data, never the person. Its one concrete UI move is a dismissible line under the nav for signed-out
visitors: "Public trading records, imported read-only from brokers. Losses included. How it works →". It flagged
"bets", "New record" and the demo handle for the owner.
*Rejected alternatives:* "Verified trades, straight from the broker." (leads with the word most open to
over-reading, and "straight from" hides that the platform computes returns); "The social network for verified
traders." (verifies people, implies skill); "Proof, not promises." (overclaims what an import proves).

**B. "Plain Ledger" (copy lead).** A careful bookkeeper reading the ledger aloud. Six voice principles (state the
fact, then stop; same words for up and down; name the limit next to the claim; plain words over finance words;
"you" for the owner, "this record" for everyone else; one name per thing), a tone map per surface, a binding
glossary and a 48-row fix list that builds the owner's F-4 wording verbatim.
*Rejected alternatives:* a friendlier, exclamatory social voice (violates "same words for up and down"); a formal
legal voice everywhere (fails the one-glance test).

**C. "Ledger Ink" (visual identity designer).** A record book, not a trading terminal. Ink is the identity and the
primary action colour; colour is spent only on meaning: blue-green gain, vermilion loss, plum demo, amber
attention, blue focus. Verified is ink, never gain-coloured. A full token set with computed contrast, a type scale
of 8 steps in system fonts, 8 spacing steps, 3 radii, text-glyph and inline-SVG icons, a single token source for app
and images, and a migration order that fixes AA failures first.
*Rejected alternatives:* keeping the current saturated green/rose with a violet accent (generic, CVD-weak, trade
dress risk); blue/orange gain/loss (best CVD separation but breaks the reader's up=green habit and collides with a
blue or violet accent).

**D. "Steady Hand" (motion stylist).** Motion only shows where something went and confirms an action took; it
never comments on whether a number is good. Tokens (0 / 120 / 160 / 240 ms, no overshoot), a global reduced-motion
block, per-interaction rules, a testable banned list and a gain/loss motion parity test. Adds the missing SW-1 hint
and focus handling for the pane.
*Rejected alternatives:* a "delight" layer (spring easings, heart bursts): reads as celebration of a trade outcome
(F6).

**E. "Legible by Default" (accessibility specialist).** The record is a public document anyone must be able to read.
The signature becomes the sign, the arrow and the word, with colour third. Contrast pairs as tests, a field-border
token, a flat state, minimum type sizes (web and cards), per-item image alt text, scroll padding for fixed bars,
and a two-tone focus ring.
*Rejected alternatives:* blue/orange as the default palette (kept as a fallback only).

**F. "Provenance first" (trust and safety reviewer).** Every pixel that shows a number carries its source, its date
and its way home, because the dangerous object is a cropped image of a big number next to "Verified" in a group
chat. Provenance line format, crop-resistant card elements, Demo as the loudest state, TR-5 rendered everywhere,
handle over display name, thesis attribution and written-at stamp, reserved handles, display-name glyph stripping,
report action, moderation additions.
*Rejected by the judges as written:* renaming the badge to "Broker-imported" (the F-4 decision keeps "Verified");
a bare "Frozen ‹date›" badge (TR-5 says "Disconnected ‹date›"); "full record" and "Only this page is the record"
in proposed strings (completeness and authority claims).

**G. "Guardrails" (legal reviewer).** The company publishes ledgers; it never cheers, forecasts or ranks
securities. A master never-list (docs only), required elements per surface (exact disclaimer next to the first
performance block; stock pages facts only), share-card rules, and name and logo rules until clearance (text
wordmark only, name in one constant).
*Rejected by the judges as written:* rewriting the profile chart to the card's running sum as a "fix" (CH-1 is a
DESIGN Must and an owner choice, so it is an owner question, §7); "sample" as a second demo word; "Every …
full trade history" in its own fix #12.

**H. "Receipt" (growth marketer).** The card is a receipt for a record: three facts, its source and the way back.
A size-only hierarchy inside F-4, crop-safe zones per size, the printed link in the text colour, one
title/description template per page, a static image for `/`, signed-out landing copy and first-party measurement
(including fixing the share-saved mislabel and splitting the share rate by record sign).
*Rejected by the judges as written:* leading the stock image with an average and range (still a security-level
score, F3b); descriptions ending with only "Not investment advice." (both sentences are required); "Never sizes"
(false once F-5 ships opt-in position size); spreading "bets" and bare "likely" to the month card.

### 6.2 Voice and tone rules

Six rules, each with a do and a don't drawn from the real copy.

1. **State the fact, then stop.** Number first, no adjectives on results.
   - Do (keep): "You're in. Next: connect your brokerage." (`lib/copy.ts:58`; only "brokerage" changes, glossary).
   - Don't: "A long, consistent record." as a gains-only line (`lib/confidence.ts:74`), which praises one sign.
2. **Same words for up and down.** Swapping a loss for a gain changes the number and the colour, never the warmth.
   - Do: the owner's 2026-10-31 table ("…with a clear pattern of gains." / "…with a clear pattern of losses.").
   - Don't: "Still early." for gains and "Still early, and results could go either way." for losses
     (`lib/confidence.ts:69`).
3. **Name the limit next to the claim.** Verified sits one tap from its definition; an average sits beside its
   range; a subset (one trade, one month) links to the record.
   - Do (keep): "See their record, wins and losses →" (`app/ui.tsx:56`).
   - Don't: a Verified badge whose meaning lives only in a tooltip (`app/ui.tsx:9`).
4. **Plain words over finance words.** Explain a technical term in place, or don't show it.
   - Do (keep): "Each closed trade is one sell, matched to earlier buys of the same symbol in the same account,
     first in, first out." (`lib/copy.ts:268`).
   - Don't: "profit factor" with no gloss (`app/u/[handle]/page.tsx:236`); "within noise"
     (`app/me/record/page.tsx:241`).
5. **"You" for the owner, "this record" for everyone else.** Contractions in interface chrome, none in legal,
   consent or disclaimer lines.
   - Do (keep): "Your record stays public if you disconnect; deleting your profile removes it." (`lib/copy.ts:74`).
   - Don't: "We could not send your link. Try again." beside "Couldn't load more." (`lib/copy.ts:28` vs `:324`).
6. **One name per thing.** A synonym is a bug (§6.3).
   - Do: "Demo" everywhere.
   - Don't: a "Demo" badge explained as "Sample data, not from a real broker." (`app/ui.tsx:10`).

**Never-list (docs only; product copy simply never uses these):** wagering and hype words; advice and acting words

### 6.6 Motion rules

1. Motion shows where something went or confirms an action; it never reacts to a number's value or sign.
2. Gains and losses are motion-identical. No CSS rule may select on `.up`, `.down`, `.loss` with a transition,
   animation or transform (a grep test), and a gain card and a loss card yield identical `getAnimations()`.
3. The finger is the clock: direct manipulation follows 1:1; only the settle after release animates.
4. Tokens: state swaps 0 ms; opacity fades 120 ms; surfaces enter 160 ms (fade plus at most an 8 px rise) and exit
   120 ms; settle after a finger lifts 160 ms; programmatic pane open capped at 240 ms. Easing out
   `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`, in `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)`. No overshoot, spring or bounce; nothing over 400 ms.
5. One global `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` block zeroes every transform, smooth scroll and animation as a
   safety net; information never disappears with the motion.
6. **Banned:** celebration of any kind (confetti, particles, glow, shine, trophy reveals); count-up or rolling of
   any figure; interpolated frames between chart ranges (they show returns that never existed) and line draw-on;
   shake or flash on errors or losses; looping or perpetual motion (pulsing dots, bobbing hints, shimmer,
   auto-advance); haptics or sound on trade content; parallax and 3D; signature motion imitating a known product.
7. **SW-1 hint:** a static line on the first card, "Swipe ← for profile", removed after the first swipe or →,
   remembered on the device. An optional single 24 px peek, first visit only, never under reduced motion.
8. Keyboard → and "← Back" move focus to the pane's name link (and back) with `preventScroll`.
9. "Link copied" holds 4 s or until pointer or focus leaves, whichever is later. "Loading…" appears only after
   300 ms and then stays at least 500 ms.
10. The reject-swipe card gets an opaque background so nothing shows through mid-drag.
