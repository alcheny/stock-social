## 2. Executive summary

- **What main proves today.** A trader can go from dev sign-in to a public, broker-sourced record in about 20-25 s on
  the vendor fake (simulation). The record carries losses ranked like gains, a 95% range, an evidence tier, open
  positions, exclusion counts and the disclaimer. A signed-out viewer can read it at 360 px with no console or HTTP
  errors on any route and no horizontal scroll. The share unfurl is honest. Dark mode is clean.
- **Where it breaks.** The weak point is not the screens. It is the record pipeline's edges: rows that leave the
  matcher without a trace, or change the figures, when the vendor data is irregular or a trader acts on purpose.
- **Top 5 findings.**
  1. **C3-1:** the importer silently drops a sell with no price, units or date. The lot stays open and misprices the
     next round trip (fictional probe: +20% published as -76%).
  2. **C3-2 and C3-3:** a 1-share transfer or a 1-share oversell turns a recorded loss into an exclusion count (the
     transfer under the wrong cause). A whole transfer out leaves no trace at all. Both amend decided rules: proposals.
  3. **C3-4:** the swipe pane, which SW-1 makes the main record view, prints the average with no 95% range (TR-9
     Must) and never shows "New record" (TR-7 Must).
  4. **C3-5:** one empty vendor account list stamps a connection "disconnected" forever, and the "frozen" record
     keeps syncing. It is invisible today and becomes a false public label when fix #3 or F-11 merges.
  5. **C3-10:** /how says the feed ignores a return's size, but the base order uses size, recency and evidence weight
     (POLICY F1: "ordered by the documented method").
- **Headline numbers.** Passes 1-2 raised 66 findings (63 from lenses, 3 new in cross-review). After merges, **47
  survive**. **15 are major**: 9 defects and 6 proposals. **32 are minor**: 18 defects and 14 proposals. On top of
  those, 8 are routed to an owner, a committee or a design session, and 6 are dropped (4.3). Fixes list: **27 items
  (F3-1..F3-27)**. Proposal briefs: **18**. Owner questions: **10**.
- **For the council.** Top proposals: C3-2 (transfers), C3-3 (oversells), C3-17 (exclusion counts beside the
  headline), C3-15 (disconnect in the app), C3-9 (broker corrections). Nothing here is a blocker today.
- **For the owner.** Section 7: ten questions, each with a recommended answer. Q1-Q3 (freeze rule, open positions
  of a disconnected account, raw-row immutability) unblock the most work.

## 3. What held up

- **Ownership.** Every server action and route reads the session user, never a client-sent id (RD). Cron routes
  compare secrets in constant time. The redirects checked are fixed paths.
- **Headers.** Nonce CSP, `frame-ancestors 'none'`, session cookies httpOnly and SameSite=Lax. Spoofed Host headers
  are not reflected (RD).
- **Held theses** are absent from every public surface checked: feed, profile, pane, pane chart, trade page, stock
  page, story image and og image (RD, WT persona 3).
- **Losses.** Feed size term uses `abs(return_pct)` (`lib/feed.ts:114`); no sign-dependent sort in discovery (RT).
  Charts always include 0% (RT). Max drawdown carries the qualifier on the profile (WT).
- **Consent and connect.** The consent screen lists exactly what becomes public and private and states the 24-hour
  limit; paper accounts are excluded and counted; the portal has a new-page fallback (WT p2-360-consent.png).
- **Demo labelling** on profile, card, pane, trade, month, stock page and og image (WT persona 4).
- **Performance.** Pages render in 10-70 ms warm on demo data; every hot read has an index; no N+1 found; type check
  clean (EQ). The one costly route is the profile share image (about 0.37 s per request, C3-35).
- **Accessibility basics.** Focus ring visible everywhere walked, no keyboard traps, menus close on Esc, reduced
  motion respected, contrast passes except items already in flight (UX). Two-step delete is safe (WT).

## 9. Known limits of this record

- **Billing is not live** on the shared app, so hosted test checkout was not walked; the paywall half of BZ is read
  from code and the F-3 e2e spec.
- **Vendor behaviour is unverified** on every point that matters to C3-1, C3-5, C3-7, C3-8, C3-9 and C3-16: whether
  trade dates are day-precision, whether empty or partial account lists happen transiently, how corrections, cancels
  and disabled authorizations appear, and whether error bodies carry personal data. Each goes to the vendor question
  list (MRC2-10).
- **No real broker was connected.** Import times (about 20-25 s) come from the vendor fake and one unexplained case
  where a 40-activity import still showed "importing" after 60 s until a reload (not investigated).
- **axe-core is not installed**, so the accessibility pass used computed-colour walks, `ariaSnapshot`, Tab walks and
  forced failure paths instead of an automated rule engine. C3-30 is code-read only.
- **Shared database.** Agents of this and earlier runs left test users (fixture traders and probe accounts) that show on
  public surfaces (a leftover handle rotates in the feed; a fixture trader appears under "Similar trades"; one stock
  baseline grew from 84 to 120 trades). Clean them up before any screenshot is used publicly.
- **Screenshots** show demo traders and this run's test handles only (`cycle3_trader`, `cycle3_trader_b`); every figure
  in them is demo or fake-vendor data.
- **Demo data** has 3 traders and 90 closes, so performance numbers measure render cost, not scale.
- **One test run.** The unit suite ran once (1,056 tests; 7 failures are C3-33); no flake data.
- Desk research is from search summaries, not full reads, and is used for context only.
