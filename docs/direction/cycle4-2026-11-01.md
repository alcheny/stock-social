## 2. Executive summary

- **What main proves today.** Three kinds of record now share one product: broker-imported share records (Verified),
  options that are imported and counted but shown as counts only, and public-filing records behind F-7's switch. The
  trust labelling holds across all three: "Excluding options" on every headline surface checked, "Public filing" never
  Verified, held theses absent from every public surface, cycle-3 fixes F3-1, F3-3, F3-4 and F3-5 working as
  specified. Signed out at 320-1280 px: no horizontal scroll, no page errors, 0 contrast failures in light and dark.
  The unit suite is green with a database (1,792 pass, 0 fail), and the C3-33 secret failures are gone.
- **Where it breaks.** At the seams the new features opened: the matcher's ordering guesses (shares and options), the
  F-7 path skipping safety rules users get, completeness and source claims that predate options and F-7, and the
  week-8 readout counting the wrong populations. One engineering regression is the most urgent single item.
- **Top 5 findings.**
  1. **C4-3:** #65 made `lib/moderation.ts` import the compliance scanner, so 8 server functions now ship most of the
     repository (130-162 MB each, mostly screenshots under `docs/`), growing about 7 MB per cycle. One config line
     removes it.
  2. **C4-1 and C4-2:** two matcher guesses publish wrong Verified figures in fictional probes: F3-5's buy-first rule
     turns a date-only same-day short into a +7.1% long trade, and the lot that covers a short starts fresh and
     prices a later long trade at +28.6% instead of -10%. Both amend recorded rulings (owner Q1).
  3. **C4-4, C4-20, C4-31:** the F-15 readout cannot be read as decided: public-filer views count toward G3 (and demo
     fixture filers escape the demo exclusion the readout states), invite views on Demo profiles vanish, and nothing
     says the counts can be inflated.
  4. **Before switch-on:** filer names and handles skip the identity rules users get (C4-11), the home preview and
     /how describe the feed wrongly in opposite directions (C4-12, C4-27), "last checked" can lie (C4-13), filers can
     be grouped by the stock a reader reads (C4-28). Owner Q2 bundles these with a counts-only dry run (C4-30).
  5. **Options claims:** the month recap says "All trades this month" over a list without option closes (C4-9); the
     profile unfurl text drops "excluding options" that the card carries (C4-10); /how says option results wait on
     the trader's consent when no trader can consent (C4-26).
- **Headline numbers.** Passes 1-2 raised 58 findings (55 from lenses, 3 new in cross-review). After merges, **43
  survive**. **5 are major**: 1 defect and 4 proposals. **38 are minor**: 20 defects and 18 proposals. On top of those,
  5 are routed to the owner, a committee, the vendor list or an in-flight branch, and 5 raised parts are dropped as

- **For the council.** Top proposals: C4-2 (cover lot), C4-1 (same-instant scope), C4-4 (G3 split), C4-31 (readout

## 3. What held up

- **Options never reach the headline.** No feed, ranking, stats, chart, TR-9 or stock-page query reads
  `option_trades`; the one display reader selects counts only. "Excluding options" appears on the card strip, pane,
  profile, chart caption, trade and month strips, share pills and alt text (RT, PD, WT on a fictional options trader).
  No strike, contract symbol or "Call option" in any owner or public HTML at 360 or 1280 (WT).
- **The options consent gate is fail-closed** (`lib/options-load.ts:17-37`); `optionsPublic()` is false today (RT,
  RD). Option math is sound on clean inputs: multiplier in the key, exercise and assignment counted, never priced.
- **F-3 cycle fixes held.** F3-1 stores and counts unusable rows that have a symbol; F3-3 needs two successful lists 24 h
  apart and never reads a frozen connection; F3-4 replaces positions per account for shares and options (RT).
- **F-7 is kept apart from Verified.** Closes are inserted `verified: false` (`lib/public-trades.ts:103`); stock pages,
  compare, theses and the stock image filter `filing_url is null`; the 30-day feed floor and noindex hold; filing links
  are origin-checked and open with `noopener noreferrer`; `pf_` is refused at sign-up, rename and dev sign-in. The F-7
  matcher reconciles each row against the filing's held-after figure, which protects it from the guesses in C4-1 and
  C4-2 (RT probes F1-F4, RD, WT).
- **F-9.** Both WT probes (a direct contact line and a spelled-out address) were held, the author saw the neutral line
  and the rule, and signed out nothing showed. Report answers one neutral "not found" for held, missing or
  non-published notes (no oracle) and keeps focus on the button (UX, RD).
- **F-13 and SW-1.** Dismissal moves focus to card 1's link, the hint waits for the line, → opens the pane and retires
  the hint, reduced motion holds, nothing overlaps at 320-1280 (UX, WT, PD).
- **F-15 privacy shape.** No address, agent, cookie or viewer id stored; closed `via` registry; cross-site beacons get
  403; the rollup is one idempotent upsert; the purge batches (RD, BZ, EQ). Share appends its class and announces
  "Link copied" (WT).
- **Engineering.** Type check clean; 1,813 tests (1,792 pass, 0 fail, 20 skipped for LOAD, 1 todo) with the database;
  no flake in reruns of the timing-sensitive files; pages render in 10-40 ms warm; every new error line goes through
  `logSafe`; the schema stays additive; cron stages are isolated; `/api/health` answers in 6-9 ms (EQ).
- **Accessibility.** 0 contrast failures after the #71 retune in light and dark on every route walked; no horizontal
  scroll at 320 px; text spacing clips nothing; a visible focus ring on every Tab stop; a refused handle change moves
  focus to the field with the rule named (UX).
- **Cost.** Options add no vendor calls per sync (rows ride the activity and positions calls); the token-budget
  governor passes 222/222 tests and 3,000 random fictional snapshots with no throw or non-determinism (BZ, EQ).

## 9. Known limits of this record

- **No real broker was connected.** Every import ran against the vendor fake; import timings and row shapes come from
  it. **Vendor behaviour is unverified** on every point that matters to C4-1, C4-6 and C4-7: whether trade dates are
  day-precision, whether share rows ever arrive with a null symbol, and how expiry, exercise and assignment rows are
  dated and stamped. Each goes to the vendor question list (MRC2-10).
- **No filer data from a real filings source.** F-7 surfaces were seen only on temporary fictional demo fixtures
  (WT's and UX's), deleted afterwards; PD, MK, CS and XR-B read them in code. RD-1's impact depends on what owner names
  the production source emits. The F-7 importer was not run end to end.
- **F-7 ran on the sandbox default.** The walk's environment file set `PUBLIC_RECORDS_CONTACT` twice and the empty
  value won, so F-7 was on through the non-production default, not through `PUBLIC_TRADERS=1` with contacts as in
  production (`lib/public-gate.ts:15-19`); `/public-records` showed "Requests open before launch."
  (p7-360-public-records.png). The correction flow was not walked. Not raised as a defect: production requires both
  contacts.
- **axe-core is not installed.** The accessibility pass used accessibility-tree snapshots, one browser accessibility
  tree query, Tab walks, computed-colour walks, a text-spacing override and reflow; one browser engine only (C4-35's
  effect depends on the browser). The author's held view (C4-18) was walked by WT and code-read by UX; the open options
  gate UI cannot be reached and was code-read.
- **The beacon sends nothing from automated browsers** (`app/view-beacon.tsx:27-29`), so no walkthrough beacon was
  observed; RD's forged beacons were curl requests, removed afterwards.
- **Shared database.** Lenses created users with `c4_` handles and temporary filer fixtures and deleted them with their
  rows (count checks: 0 left). EQ found and deleted 15 users and drop rows leaked by a DB test (C4-23) and 5 drop rows
  from its own probe. Demo trade ids changed during the pass as other lenses re-seeded, so demo figures can differ
  slightly between screenshots.
- **Screenshots** show demo traders, fictional `c4_wt_*` users and a fictional demo filer only. The demo seed uses
  real-looking tickers (pre-existing); this record names none.
- **One test run with a database:** 1,813 tests, 1,792 pass, 0 fail, 20 skipped (LOAD=1), 1 todo; plus one run without
  a database (1,440 pass, 372 skipped) and two reruns of four timing-sensitive files. No e2e run (by the brief).
- **Bundle sizes** (C4-3) come from the running build's trace files at ff25ad5; 3f216ba was not rebuilt to compare.
  Serverless cold starts were not measured.
- **Billing is not live**, so checkout was not walked; C4-41 is read from code and tests. Vendor and processor pricing
  were not re-verified (the $1.03 per trader per month figure is the business council's).
- **Desk research** for C4-30 is Low confidence (one vendor's single-day sweep, generic statute) and used for context
  only; the dry run replaces it.
