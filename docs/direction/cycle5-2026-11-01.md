## 1. Method

The record has three passes. Each member of each pass was a separate agent with fresh context.

1. **Lenses, 5 agents in parallel.** All five got the same base brief: audit main as it is now, which since cycle 4
   includes #85 (F-11 and F3-2), #87 (F-26), #77 (F-20), #89 (the cycle-4 fixes: the oversell gap rule, a deploy
   rebuild, moderation v4, server bundle tracing), #92 (the visible-users predicate), #93 (F-27), and #88, #94 and #90
   (tests and the likes lookup). They were told not to raise again anything on cycle 4's lists, in the owner brief or
   on an in-flight branch. Each diffed every in-flight branch (`git diff origin/main...origin/claude/<b>`) before
   calling a finding new, and cited `path:line` re-read with `sed -n`.
   - **UX design and walkthrough.** A production build at 320, 360 and 1280 px:
     - a signed-out viewer from profile, trade and month links (with and without `?via=card`), the feed and pane,
       every static page and every link-preview image;
     - a first-time trader from `/login/dev` through the vendor fake to an imported record, notes (one held), settings,
       `/me/record` and profile deletion;
     - dark mode.

     They checked every Stage 5 Must against what renders, and took 28 screenshots.
   - **RT record trust.** It tried to make a record lie, with pure probes on `lib/trades.ts` and end to end through the
     vendor fake on its own database and server. Every public figure was checked by hand against the raw rows.
   - **MK market and business.** Desk research (about 14 searches, retrieved 2026-11-01, cited generically: POLICY T1-T5
     allow no third-party names) plus a read of the product copy and measurement.
   - **EQ engineering.** It generated a 3.3 GB fictional dataset (50,000 users, 3.0 M activities, 2.0 M closed trades,
     heavy-tailed), captured every statement real page renders ran, and ran `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)` on each.
     It also tested rebuild crash safety, a security header and CSRF pass, two full unit runs, server traces and the
     ponytail inventory.
   - **AX accessibility.** A manual WCAG 2.2 AA pass (no automated accessibility rule engine is installed, and no dependency was added):
     accessibility-tree snapshots, Tab walks, computed contrast in light and dark, reflow at 320 and 400% zoom, text
     spacing, forced colours, reduced motion, target size and the drag alternatives. 3 screenshots.
2. **Synthesis (this pass).** The chair re-read the cited lines of every major finding at 56421b9 (RT-1, RT-2 on
   `claude/f24-late-postings`, EQ-1, EQ-2, EQ-3, UX-1, MK-1, AX-1 and the two branch hazards; RT-3 and MK-4 were
   re-read by pass 3, which corrected their lines), applied cycle 4's class
   rule, merged duplicates, and routed what an owner question or an in-flight branch already owns. The rule: a defect
   (D) breaks a written rule or decision and goes to the fixes list; a proposal (P) is a new rule, or amends a recorded
   ruling, and goes to the ballot.
3. **Red team of this synthesis.** A separate agent re-read the evidence lines and challenged severities, classes and
   coverage; its changes are listed in section 9.

**Early hand-offs (during pass 1).** Two items touched branches in review or close to merge, so the chair sent them to
the orchestrating session at once, with the evidence and acceptance checks. The orchestrator confirmed both and
forwarded them:
- C5-2 to the F-24 session, as a blocker on #84 ("#84 stays unmerged until that's verified");
- the trade-page metadata hazard to both the F-16/F-17 and F-25 sessions.

Section 7 records both.

## 2. Executive summary

- **What main proves today.** The cycle-4 trust fixes hold under attack.
  - #89's oversell gap counted every later sell in every variant RT probed: renames, splits on a gapped pair, a 1e-8
    residual. It did not leak across accounts.
  - Every figure on a plain fictional record matched a hand computation from the raw rows: win rate, average, range,
    drawdown, compounded end, fees, a 2:1 split, the stock page share and the month count.
  - Held notes are absent from HTML, RSC payloads, pane JSON and images. Demo and Public filing are never Verified.
    `closed_trades` stores no prices or quantities.
  - Signed out at 320 to 1280 px: no horizontal scroll, no console errors, 0 contrast failures in light and dark.
  - C4-3's trace fix holds: the largest server function is down from 130-162 MB to 40.6 MB.
  - Unit suite with a database: 2,223 tests, 2,195 pass, 0 fail, 26 skipped (LOAD), 2 todo (owner items). Two runs
    gave identical results.
- **Where it breaks.** At the edges of the matcher (transfers, held re-issues), at scale (ticker pages and the home
  feed do whole-table work on every request), at the seam between a rebuild and a write (notes), and in a few places
  where copy or a surface says less, or more, than the record holds.
- **Top 5 findings.**
  1. **C5-1:** a one-share transfer of a stock can remove a loss on that stock in that account, closed up to 14 days
     earlier. The vendor's transfer rows become unpriceable actions, which open the 14-day late-posting window
     backwards over earlier sells of the pair. Fictional end to end: win rate 80% → 100%, average −5.4% → +8.3%,
     drawdown → 0.0%, still Verified. The trader steers it after seeing the loss. The window is a committee ruling and
     transfers are OD 2.28's question, so this is a ballot item (pass 3).
  2. **C5-2 (in flight, #84):** F-24's held "possible re-issues" are dropped from the rebuild and counted only in an
     owner log. A held twin sell makes a −40% close vanish, and the next sell is then published at −60% instead of
     +33.3%. Handed off as a merge blocker; the fix has since landed on the branch, awaiting verification.
  3. **C5-3:** a note saved from a page loaded before any rebuild is silently lost. The form posts
     `closed_trades.id`, which every rebuild renumbers (reproduced). A rebuild runs whenever a sync stores a new
     activity, about daily for an active trader.
  4. **C5-6, C5-7:** at 2 M closes, each `/t/SYMBOL` request scans the whole table (10.8-14.4 s). Ten anonymous
     requests exhaust the 10-connection pool. The signed-out home feed re-ranks on every visit (2.45-2.63 s), because
     page 1's cache key contains the current millisecond.
  5. **C5-4:** ten trades of exactly +1% get a zero-width range, "Past results: above zero" and a higher feed weight
     (0.299) than a 200-bet record (0.167); ten exact −1% trades weigh 0.302. That defeats TR-9's "small lucky samples
     can't top it".
- **Headline numbers.**
  - Lenses raised 47 numbered items plus 4 notes promoted to rows (C5-23, C5-42, C5-43, C5-44). After merges,
    **44 survive**:
    - **10 major**: 6 defects and 4 proposals;
    - **34 minor**: 20 defects and 14 proposals.
  - On top of those, 6 rows in 4.2 route 9 lens items to the owner brief or an in-flight branch (C5-2 is ranked and
    also handed off), and the "still reproduces" lists are in 4.3.
  - Fixes list: **25 items (F5-2, F5-4..F5-27; F5-1 and F5-3 moved to the ballot in pass 3)**. Proposal briefs:
    **22** (six of them new feature proposals from MK, labelled MK-P1..MK-P6).
  - Hand-offs: **6**, plus 2 notes. New owner questions: **4**.
- **For the council.** The top proposals:
  - C5-1: transfers leave the backward window (it waits on OD 2.28 and one vendor answer).
  - C5-4: an evidence floor for ranking (owner Q3).
  - C5-5: one open-positions line on every surface (it waits on OD 2.25).
  - C5-10: the forward record, trades opened after connecting shown apart (it waits on OD 2.7).
  - C5-32: a check code on every share picture.
  - C5-30: publish a trade only once its date has passed.
  - C5-31: fees in the return's denominator (owner Q1).
- **For the owner.** Section 8 has four new questions:

## 3. What held up

- **Record math.** No figure on any public surface differed from the hand check on RT's plain fictional record:
  profile, pane, card, trade and month pages, `/t`, and the images. The feed sorts by `abs(return_pct)`. The importer
  counts zero, negative and invalid prices and units instead of pricing them. Rows dated more than 24 hours ahead are
  quarantined and counted on their own line (RT).
- **#89's oversell rule** (C4-2's fix) holds for later buys then a round trip, a rename carrying lots, a rename and
  back, a split on a gapped pair, and a tiny residual. "2 sells excluded…" showed end to end (RT).
- **Trust labels.** Demo shows on every surface; Verified requires every source row from the vendor; public filings
  keep their own badge and are stored unverified; a held note never shows to a reader on any surface or payload (RT,
  UX).
- **Rebuild atomicity.** A reader polling every 0.5 s through a 30 s held-open rebuild saw the same 1,113 rows
  throughout, and still saw them after a `kill -9` of the rebuilding process. The deploy, one-off and sync rebuilds
  share the per-trader advisory lock (EQ).
- **Security posture.**
  - Real sessions: a random token, an HMAC-signed cookie, sha256 at rest, httpOnly and sameSite=lax. Only `/login/dev`
    is a stub, and it refuses in production.
  - CSP uses a per-request nonce with `strict-dynamic`, `frame-ancestors 'none'`, nosniff and frame denial.
  - A server action posted with a foreign `Origin` was refused, and `/api/sync` gives 401 without the bearer.
  - The 4 `unsafe` SQL calls carry constant text only, and errors expose a digest only.

  CLAUDE.md's "auth is a dev stub" line is stale; that is OD launch row 1, cited (EQ).
- **Earlier fixes holding.** C4-3's trace exclusion: no server trace includes `docs/`, `e2e/` or `team/`. C4-23's test
  cleanup: `lib/f15-views.db.test.ts` leaves nothing. #94's likes lookup: 20,034 index probes at 11 µs each. #92's
  predicate: about 14 ms (EQ).
- **Design.**
  - CH-1 passes on all its Musts and the accessible label.
  - TR-1, TR-2, TR-3, TR-7, TR-8 and TR-9 pass as rendered.
  - SW-1 passes for keyboard, reduced motion, return to the same card and the name link.
  - Connect, consent and import have no dead end and end on a populated profile with a Share box.
  - No celebration on real trades; losses keep their sign, the word "Loss" and the down colour everywhere.
  - Pages reach network idle in 0.7-1.3 s at demo scale (UX).
- **Accessibility.**
  - Contrast: 0 text failures in light and dark at every width; control boundaries and chart lines meet 3:1.
  - Reflow: none at 320 × 256.
  - Focus: a visible ring on every Tab stop; the skip link is the first stop; → opens the pane and moves focus into
    it; Back returns focus to the card.
  - Announcements: Follow, Share and Report announce.
  - The swipe and the chart scrub have non-drag alternatives (2.5.7) (AX).
