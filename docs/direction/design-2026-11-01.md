## How the council worked

**Members.** The members were a product designer (PD), an interaction designer (IX: swipe feed, panes, gestures), a
data-visualization designer (DV: CH-1/CH-2), a mobile and responsive specialist (MR: 360 px first), an accessibility
specialist (A11Y: WCAG 2.2 AA), a trust and record-integrity designer (TR: TR-1..TR-9), an information architect (IA), a
design-systems engineer (DS: tokens in `app/globals.css`), a red team (RT) and the chair. Each member was a separate agent
with fresh context.

**Five passes.**
1. **Walkthrough.** The app was a production build with dev auth and the in-repo vendor fake. Every route was shot at
   360 and 1,280 px, light and dark, in three roles: signed out, signed in as a viewer with no broker, and signed in as a
   trader who connected through the vendor fake. The trader also covered the connect states S1–S8: S1 sign-in and
   attestation, S2 connect page, S3 portal dialog, S4 consent, S5 importing, S6 declined or no real account, S7 abandoned
   or not-found, S8 empty record. The demo traders and one seeded 60-trade connected record served as data. The pass
   produced 116 full-page screenshots.
2. **Independent lens reviews.** Each lens wrote principles, 13–19 verified issues (147 in all), 0–3 proposals (22 in all)
   and sources. Members took their own crops and measurements with Playwright: computed styles, contrast math, Tab walks
   and reflow at 320×256 and 780×360.
3. **Cross-review.** Each lens reviewed two others' lists (agree, dispute, duplicate or needs evidence). They re-checked
   cheap claims against the app and code and listed missed issues and conflicts with POLICY, Stage 5 or owner decisions.
4. **Severity panel.** The panel merged everything into D-01..D-91 and spot-checked the blockers in code. It resolved
   twelve conflicts without the owner, raised the owner questions and merged the 22 proposals into 19 entries.
5. **Chair and red team.** The chair wrote the direction, the principles, the design-system direction and ten proposals.
   The red team then attacked the chair's text and the panel. Its 13 required and 7 optional revisions are recorded under
   "Red-team pass" below.

**Screenshots.** Pass-1 shots are in `screens-2026-11-01/`, named `<role>-<screen>-<width>-<scheme>.png`. The roles are
`out` (signed out), `viewer` (signed in, no broker) and `trader` (connected through the vendor fake, with `S2`..`S8` for

**Committed evidence only.** To keep the record light, only the 15 screenshots that evidence a blocker or a proposal are

## Direction

**What the app gets right.** The core promise is built honestly: broker-sourced records only, no manual trade path, losses
ranked like gains, every close reachable (TR-1 passes on every surface), the headline is all-time, sells without an entry are
excluded and counted, charts are percent-only with 0% on the axis, and the swipe feed works signed out. The dark/light token
base is complete inside the app and motion is already minimal.

**What matters most.** Four themes cover almost every blocker:
1. **User text must never speak for the platform** (D-01, D-02, D-08, D-33). Moderation publishes promotional phrases, links
   and spoofed "verified" claims; renamed handles can be taken over at once. This is an abuse hole, not polish.
2. **Every number carries its record, its trust state and its date** (D-03, D-04, D-05, D-09, D-11, D-12, D-35, D-42, D-43).
   Crops, cards, panes and images drop the disclaimer, open positions, the year, freshness and the disconnected state.
3. **One number, one definition** (D-10, D-28, D-45, D-23, D-24, D-48). The headline average sits outside its own range,
   which also ignores open positions; zero is red in one place and green in another; a month earns a verdict the record doesn't.
4. **The keyboard, zoom and landscape paths match the finger path** (D-13..D-17, D-37, D-40, D-41).

**Order of work.**
- **Now, before any public deploy** (not blocked by sessions): P-1 thesis safety, P-2 handle integrity, P-3 trust language
  (badge text, contrast, Demo naming; hue waits on OD-5 but the copy doesn't), P-4 parts on profile/trade/month/`/t`
  (disclaimer in view, year on dates, Disconnected status line), D-24 month verdict removed, P-5 `tone()` and dates,
  D-07 baseline wording (OD-8),
  D-14..D-16 contrast and focus, D-18/D-19 titles and 404s.
- **After in-flight sessions merge:** F-4 rework → D-06, D-10, D-23, image halves of D-34/D-35, D-87. F-6 → D-11, D-48
  (blocker: open positions in the range, TR-9), D-25, D-46, D-84. F-2 → P-6 (feed card, pane, gestures, card strip D-42, in-feed disclaimer D-09). F-3 → P-7, P-8. Privacy → D-39 (OD-6).
- **Later:** P-10 full token migration and ratchets, month index and trader search (P-9 tail), interval strip, desktop
  layout (OD-11, kept deferred).

## Design principles

1. **The record comes before the trade.** Any surface that shows one trade, one month or one image also shows the whole
   record it belongs to. *Test:* every trade/month page, feed card and image shows "1 of N", average with range and open positions.
2. **User text is never the platform speaking.** Theses, names and handles are normalised, attributed and held when they
   promise, link out or claim verification. *Test:* the bypass corpus (bidi, zero-width, look-alikes, URLs) is all held.
3. **Uncertainty travels with the number, and the headline sits inside it.** *Test:* `low ≤ headline ≤ high` on every
   seeded record; no average appears without its range or "too few bets".
4. **Each colour has one meaning; sign is carried by glyph or word first.** Up/down mean P&L only; trust, warning, action,
   focus and flat get their own tokens. *Test:* no failure state uses `--down`; 0.0% reads "Flat" in neutral.
5. **Every crop states its own scope, source and date.** Trust state (Verified from broker or Demo, equal weight), year
   when not current, "as of", Connected or Disconnected. *Test:* in the DOM, the handle, trust
   badge and date share the nearest container with the large % and sit inside its bounding box at 360 px (Playwright).
6. **Every gesture has a visible or keyboard twin, and focus follows the view.** *Test:* Tab walk reaches every card's
   record without passing hidden panes; → and Back move focus; browser back restores the card.
7. **Content decides the size, not the device.** Longest legal values, 320 px, landscape and 400% zoom never clip or scroll
   sideways; fixed chrome never covers focus or the disclaimer. *Test:* the responsive gate at 320, 360, 780×360, 320×256.
8. **Values come from scales; contrast belongs to the token pair.** *Test:* the contrast-pair unit test in both schemes and
   a ratchet on literal px values.

## Proposals

### P-1 Thesis safety v2 and attributed user notes
**Why.** Moderation publishes promotional phrases, links, signal selling and look-alike spellings (D-01); direction-control
characters let a thesis rewrite the verified number (D-02); theses look like platform copy (D-33) and an after-the-fact
note reads as pre-trade reasoning (D-49).
**Decisions proposed.**
1. Normalise before matching: NFKC, strip zero-width/format and direction-control characters (U+202A–202E, U+2066–2069,
   U+200E/F), hand-written confusable map (no new dependency).
2. HOLD, never drop: the PROMO list, URLs and domains, signals, copy-trading, picks, contact or messenger words, @contacts,
   claims naming "Stock Social", "verified" or "official", and stated return percentages.
3. Every user text renders through one `UserNote` component with attribution and a Report action. A report comes from a
   signed-in user, one per user per thesis, rate-limited, and queues the thesis for review; it is auto-held only after N
   distinct reporters; the owner sees "Held for review"; a report never touches trades (TR-1). Profiles get a Report action too (POLICY U2).
4. Store `thesis_written_at` and `thesis_edited_at` (additive columns, re-attached by `sell_id`) and show "Written ‹date›,
   after the trade closed · edited ‹date›".
5. A minimal review queue (an admin page or a CLI script) with "Publish" and "Keep held" actions, so held never means
   silently dropped (POLICY U1).
**Acceptance criteria.**
1. Every entry of the bypass corpus in `lib/moderation.test.ts` (zero-width, Cyrillic look-alike, bidi override, URL, a
   contact-me-for-tips phrase) returns `held`; a negative corpus of ordinary theses (stop-loss levels, earnings notes, a
   plain reflection on selling early) stays `published` (price-target phrasing may stay held, F1).
2. Saved thesis text contains no U+202A–202E, U+2066–2069, U+200E/F.
3. Thesis, profile row, feed card and `/t` all render the same `UserNote` markup with `<bdi>` and the attribution label.
4. A UserNote is never set larger than the verified figure on the same surface (computed style check).
5. Dates survive `rebuildTrades` (integration test) and show on public views.
6. A single report does not change `thesis_status`; N distinct signed-in reports do; signed-out users cannot report; schema change has no drop/truncate/delete.
7. A held thesis can be published from the review queue (test); every profile shows a Report action.
**Resolves.** D-01, D-02 (thesis half), D-33, D-49.
**Depends on / conflicts.** Must land before any card redesign that promotes the thesis (P-4, P-6). POLICY U1, U2, F1, F2. A
confusables library would need the owner (POLICY H).

### P-2 Handle and name integrity
**Why.** A renamed handle is free at once and can be taken by an impostor; product, staff and look-alike handles sign up and
unfurl (D-08); display names can carry a check glyph and the word "Verified" into the h1 and tab title (D-02).
**Decisions proposed.**
1. On rename, reserve the old handle 365 days (as account deletion does) and 301 `/u/old/**` (trade and month included) to the new one.
2. A reserved list (product name, staff and role words, verified, official, vendor, demo handles).
3. One look-alike rule: compute a skeleton (lower-case, rn→m, 0→o, 1→l); a new handle is refused when its skeleton, or its
   skeleton with a trailing run of digits or underscores removed, equals the skeleton of a reserved, demo or
   connected-record handle. Ordinary new handles with digits and `_` stay free.
4. Display names: strip direction controls, reject ✓/✔/☑, "verified", "official"; cap emoji.
5. Titles lead with the handle: "@h (Name) · Stock Social".
6. One rename per 30 days (new product rule, OD-12).
**Acceptance criteria.**

4. A display name containing ✔ or "Verified" is refused; tab title begins with "@".
**Resolves.** D-08, D-02 (name half), D-18 (handle-led titles).
**Depends on / conflicts.** OD-12 (reservation, 301, optional permanent retirement of handles that had a connected record, cooldown). Pairs with P-8 moving Change handle to Settings. POLICY U2.

indexable empty "trader" profile (D-39); likes and follows are three to four taps deep (D-31); the nav has no label or current state (D-75).
**Decisions proposed.**
1. Header "Sign in" plus "Publish your trades" (`?intent=publish`); contextual h1 ("Sign in to watch NVDA"); only `intent=publish` lands on `/connect`.
2. Strict same-origin `next`: single leading `/`, allow-listed `/`, `/u/…`, `/t/…`, optional `#trade-…`; reject `//`, `\`, encoded schemes.
3. A `/me` sub-nav (Following · Likes · Hidden · Settings) with `aria-current`.
4. Until a consented connection, `/u/<viewer>` is a noindex stub "No broker connected. Nothing here is verified.", absent from the sitemap (OD-6).
5. Nav `aria-label="Main"`, `aria-current`; ≤ 400 px a 44×44 "Me" link named "Your profile, @handle".
6. Connect flow: browser back from the import screen never shows a live consent form (replace-navigate or no-store); portal Esc/Close after load returns to the connect page with "Closed. Nothing was saved." and focus on the primary, S7a only for vendor closed/abandoned messages (OD-7).
**Acceptance criteria.**
1. Unit tests: `next` values `//evil`, `/\evil`, `javascript:`, `%2F%2F` are rejected; `/t/NVDA` and `/u/h#trade-x` accepted.
2. Like signed out → sign in → returns to the same card with the like applied.
3. Viewer profile has `noindex` and is absent from the sitemap.
4. Every `/me/*` page reaches the others in one tap.
5. e2e with the vendor fake: back from the import screen re-redirects; Close after the portal loads lands on the connect page with focus on the primary.
**Resolves.** D-31, D-38, D-39, D-60, D-61, D-75.
**Depends on / conflicts.** OD-6, OD-7; F-3 (viewers carry the cost, so no "for free" copy); F-2 (likes, follow); privacy session.

### P-10 Design-system foundation and its CI gate
**Why.** Only colours are tokens: 23 font sizes, 7 radii, 17 gaps, one button defined eight times, three "muted" greys in
images, 62 inline styles (D-87..D-91); contrast and boundary failures exist because pairs aren't tokens (D-13..D-15);

chart screen-reader noise (D-52); browser chrome dark in light mode (D-78).
**Decisions proposed.**

2. `lib/palette.ts` single source for images and `themeColor`.
3. Base `h1, .who { overflow-wrap:anywhere }`; global `scroll-padding`; `min-height: var(--tap)` on all controls.
4. One live region per chart, updated on key step/release only; hit layer `aria-hidden`; dates in words; under 480 px a taller breakpoint viewBox (e.g. 640×320, no `preserveAspectRatio="none"`) so CH-1 reaches ~160 px.
5. Dependency-free CI gate now; an automated rule engine only on owner approval (OD-10).
**Acceptance criteria.**

4. Playwright at 320, 360, 780×360, 320×256: no horizontal scroll with a 20-char handle and 40-char name; every control ≥ 44 px; Tab walk finds no focused element under fixed chrome.
5. Under reduced motion `--dur-fast` reads 0 ms and no transition runs after a swipe or sheet open.
6. Light-scheme `themeColor` is #fafafa.
**Resolves.** D-14, D-15, D-16, D-27, D-50, D-51, D-52, D-56, D-57, D-77, D-78, D-79, D-80, D-81, D-82, D-87, D-88, D-89, D-90, D-91.
