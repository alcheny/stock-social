# F-8 design council · prediction game · 2026-11-01

**Date:** 2026-11-01 · **Feature:** `docs/features/F-8-prediction-game.md` (Status: draft) · **Chair:** the F-8 council
session started by the owner's orchestrating session · **Design only:** no app code was written or changed.

**The owner's words:** "add idea to make stock social roguelike, like a short 3 minute session where you make trades
and try to maximize returns and predict stock price movement. or any other game-like feature rewarding good
predictions (with no real money involved). only design ideas, no implementation. iterate through 10,000 rounds and
form a committee of all the agents to synthesize the most addictive, ergonomic designs. use thesis, synthesis,
antithesis."

**How "most addictive" was read.** POLICY F5/F6 and the CLAUDE.md product rules were not changed by the owner, so
"most addictive" was read as **most engaging and replayable for adults, within those rules**. A responsible-design
seat held a veto over compulsion patterns POLICY bans (loss-chasing, variable-ratio rewards, streak guilt, timers),
and every surviving design states why players return **without** them. Scoring rules that punish overconfidence
were preferred over luck throughout.

Not investment advice. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

## 1. Result in one screen

**Recommended: F-A · Forecast Run**, a 10-room, ~3-minute roguelike run over **generated** price lines. In each room
the player says how likely the line is to end higher, as a percent on a slider (2..98). Doors between rooms choose
the next rooms' "weather", bosses ask for a second forecast, and the run summary keeps skill ("Expected for your
forecasts") apart from luck ("Luck moved this run by N points"). The score is the log score in coin-flip points,
`round(100 × (1 + log2 q))`, so 50 always scores 0 and overconfidence costs on average. A stated finish cue tells the
player when they forecast at the generator's level ("What is left is luck"), after which the game stops paying
attention to outcomes.

**Timing:** 15 of 20 committee members want F-A's first session shipped as **F-E, a dated 28-day flagged test with a
holdout and pre-written stop rules**, before the full F-A is built. Building after the DESIGN.md week-8 gates or
running F-E now is the owner's call (O3).

**Alternates:** **F-D · Practice, staged** (F-A first, then a Range run row on evidence) and **F-B · Range Run**
(10 rooms of "where does this line end?" as a low-high range, interval score). **Not recommended:** F-C · This Week's
Five (a shared weekly puzzle; needs a crowd the product will not have at week 8; 15 of 20 objected).

## 3. The dialectic, round by round

Full texts: `game-2026-11-01/dialectic/r<n>-thesis.md`, `r<n>-antithesis.md`, `r<n>-synthesis.md` (about 48,000 words).
Each role was a separate agent with fresh context; the briefs are in `game-2026-11-01/briefs/`.

### Round 1 (20 → 12)
- **Thesis.** Restated all 20 as buildable designs, fixed incoherent bred fields (e.g. a swipe input on a log score
  became a slider), and proposed a shared shell: a separate Practice area, always labelled, off the feed and
  profiles; no streaks or push; a 60-forecast floor before anything is ranked; Brier, Brier-skill and interval-score
  formulas. Strongest cases: the Dojo, scored against the generator's known chance (no outcome luck); the Gauntlet,
  whose shared seed removes card luck between players; a log-loss health bar.
- **Antithesis.** Keep 5, fix 11, kill 4 (the global skill table, opinion "voices", a Dojo duplicate, the binary
  swipe). Found scoring holes: always answering 50% passed the Dojo's top belt; a coin-flipper passed a ladder's
  unlock; run-ending rules and floors broke proper scoring; designs with no 50% input forced a view. Cross-cutting:
  per-round timers fail WCAG 2.2.1; masked historical charts can be re-identified and need a data licence; a seed is
  an answer key unless the server holds the future; daily cadence creeps in.
- **Synthesis.** 12 designs and shell v2: generated data only, futures held on the server, untimed input, 50 always
  allowed, scores compared with an always-50 baseline, claims only at pooled n ≥ 60 with a band, no floors or death
  rules, weekly cadence at most, every share labelled as a practice game.

### Round 2 (12 → 8)
- **Thesis.** The chair asked the advocate to defend the owner's literal ask (a roguelike in which you "make trades
  and try to maximize returns"). Key finding: a paper stake split by your stated chance, compounded, **is** the log
  score, so "grow paper capital" and "state your honest chance" are the same instruction; a fixed-length run with
  visible capital avoids the death-rule distortion when capital can never hit zero (2..98 clamp) and nothing reads it.
- **Antithesis.** The maths held except two slips (the "typical" fan value was a geometric pace, not a median; one
  interval figure was 4.17 instead of 4.11). **F6 ruling:** not wagering in law (nothing of value staked, no prize),
  but a run with "the house", prices, a compounding balance and payouts has the *shape* of a wager and of a
  binary-option product; responsible design vetoed it until the input is a plain chance slider, all house, price and
  payout words go, the headline is an expected pace, nothing carries across runs, and free practice has no balance.
  Also found: staking 50 against a non-50 price earned points with no view; a lift rule promoted the overconfident.
- **Synthesis.** 8 designs and shell v3: "paper call" not "trade" in the UI; a copy list the scanner enforces; no
  game score shown as a percent or a best; truth about a shared seed only after it closes. Two designs kept the
  owner's roguelike shape in veto-compliant form (Paper Call Run, Paper Ticket Run).

### Round 3 (8 → 6)
- **Thesis.** Argued from the player's first 10 seconds (business-lead principles: question, delete, simplify). The
  first screen is the first card, slider at 50, Lock the only button; session 1 has no doors, tiers, fans or jargon.
  Introduced **coin-flip points** `100 × (1 + log2 q)`: 50 scores 0, 70 scores +49 / −74, 90 scores +85 / −232.
  Points replace the paper index everywhere (same ranks, no money-like framing). Proposed "texture" doors whose value
  is variety and a one-line lesson, not score.
- **Antithesis.** Maths correct, lesson wrong: "being sure costs more than it pays" is false; the true lesson is
  "being surer than the evidence costs". Restored three deletions: the luck line ("Expected for your forecasts / This
  run"), presets for ranges (WCAG 2.5.7 needs a non-drag path), and bosses as content. The new game designer seat
  found four designs boring in session 1. Vetoed an index test arm whose keep rule rewarded return visits.
- **Synthesis.** 6 designs and shell v4: the 18+ gate on the Practice menu; Exit next to Lock; "Lower / Higher"
  slider ends; the luck line plus a raw count ("Your 70s: 4 of 6 went your way"); every design states its session-1
  hook in one sentence.

### Round 4 (6 → 5)
- **Thesis.** Argued each design as the one thing to build first and as a mode in one Practice area, with week 1–4
  arcs and 360 px wireframes. Found the six ran on two card engines (forecast card, range card).
- **Antithesis.** Added a business lead seat: no design moves a DESIGN.md week-8 gate; Practice is signed-in and 18+
  only so it cannot reach signed-out link visitors; DESIGN Stage 3 deferred predictions; build nothing before the
  gates, or one dated test with a stop rule. Wireframe faults: 26 × 20 px buttons (below WCAG 2.5.8 and the app's
  44 px rule), Lock below the fold, Exit beside Lock. Disclaimer: game points may be "performance" (owner question).
  Copy: "call" reads as an options contract, use "forecast"; countdowns vetoed for absolute close times. Session 20
  was weak: once a player's expected score nears the best, only luck varies, which drifts toward a variable reward,
  so every mode needs a finish cue. Killed four friend mutations (the product has no social graph).
- **Synthesis.** Five complete alternatives (not modes): Forecast Run, Range Run, This Week's Five, Practice staged,
  and the business lead's dated test; shell v5 (44 px targets, Exit top-left and Lock bottom-right, the full
  disclaimer on every reveal and summary, finish cues by an equivalence test, Practice only in the account menu,
  never pushed); a signed-out demo card proposal; owner questions O1–O4.

### Round 5 (5 → 5 finalists)
- **Thesis.** Checked each against the owner's words (naming its roguelike elements, or honestly labelling it the
  "other game-like feature"), stated "players return because X, not because Y", drafted acceptance criteria, and
  answered the dissent. Mutations: Lenses (choose what to see next), Two rings, the week's weather, Twin room, One door.
- **Antithesis.** Recomputed every number (Bold −88 and the range figures confirmed; the dated test's sample size
  corrected to ~900 per arm at full uptake and ~20,500 at 20% uptake; a four-option weather score fixed to log4).
  Ruled the paper-stake explanation exact but only half honest ("maximize returns" means average return, which rewards
  going all-in; the log score rewards log growth), so it stays in this design record for the owner only and never on a
  Practice page. Closed a peek-and-exit exploit (a room counts once its chart is shown). Reworded every acceptance
  criterion into a unit, property, API or Playwright check.
- **Synthesis.** Finalists F-A..F-E with shell v6 (`dialectic/r5-synthesis.md`), which section 5 adopts with the
  committee's amendments.

## 5. The recommended design: F-A · Forecast Run

Everything below is F-A from `dialectic/r5-synthesis.md` with the committee's demands folded in. Where a demand
changed the round-5 shell it says so.

### 5.1 Shared rules (shell v6, amended by the committee)
1. **Placement.** Practice is reachable only from the signed-in, 18+ attested account menu. Never in the feed, on a
   profile, a share card or beside a verified number. No push, email or badge, ever (F3b). No comment box.
2. **Data.** Generated price lines only (a seeded generator with named families: snap-back, trend, sudden jumps).
   No market data, no tickers, no dates, no real securities, so nothing can read as a call on a real stock and no
   data vendor is needed. The future of every line is held on the server until Lock.
3. **Copy.** "Forecast", never "call" or "pick"; "Best expected here", never "best possible"; distances, never
   closeness ("ended 0.4 percent inside", never "so close"). No Practice page matches
   `/\b(stake|odds|bet|wager|return|returns|payout|wealth|win|jackpot)\b/i`, relative times, "so close" or "just".
   "Points" means only the score. No jargon (bits, Brier, interval score) on cards.
4. **Strip and disclaimer.** First card of a session: "Practice game. Generated charts, not market data. No money.
   Not investment advice." Later cards: "Practice game · generated · no money" with the full strip as its
   accessible name. Every reveal and summary contains "Not investment advice. Past performance does not guarantee
   future results." once, unbroken (owner question O1 may relax reveals only).
5. **Layout.** Every control ≥ 44 × 44 px with ≥ 8 px gaps. Exit top-left, Lock bottom-right, never on one row. At
   360 × 560 Lock is in view without scrolling; at 200% text and 320 px reflow the card scrolls and never clips.
6. **Slider.** 2..98; [−1]/[+1] buttons; arrows 1, Page 10, Home/End; readout "70 percent: higher"; at 50 "50: no
   view. Scores 0 every time." Room position as text in a live region.
7. **Text alternative (committee: UX lead, accessibility).** Before Lock, the shown line is described as text (start,
   end, high, low, recent trend, in percent) as the chart's accessible description; every reveal names the end point.
8. **Colour (committee: design direction chair).** Practice never uses the gain/loss tokens (`--up`, `--down`); it has
   its own neutral tokens in `app/globals.css` meeting the contrast-pair tests in both schemes.
9. **Motion (committee: creative direction chair; replaces shell 15's 1.5 s).** Lines appear as a static frame or a
   fade of at most 400 ms; no line draw-on, walk or reveal animation; nothing decelerates near an edge; a static
   frame under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
10. **Shown counts.** A room counts once its chart is shown. Exit on an unlocked card scores that room as no view (50,
    0 points). Exit ends the run; no retry; a started seed is never reissued.
11. **Order (committee: behavioural economist).** On every reveal and summary, "Expected" and "Best expected" lines
    come before "This run" and "Luck" lines, at equal or larger size.
12. **No re-roll (committee: responsible design).** The run summary has no Play again or Next run control; its only
    actions are Done (back to the Practice menu) and Copy route. Nothing starts a run automatically.
13. **Finish cue.** After at least 60 shown rooms, when the upper 95% band of mean shortfall (mean + 1.96 × sd / √n)
    is at or below margin M (a config value set in playtest, default 5 points per room), the mode says once: "You
    forecast at the generator's level here. What is left is luck." Afterwards no "This run" or luck line appears,
    only expected lines.
14. **No social graph.** No ghosts, friends, head-to-head, seed passing or leaderboards. Seeds are personal and opaque.
15. **Report page** (never pushed): one n, plain words, bucket bands only at n ≥ 60, a table view, export and delete.
16. **Privacy (committee: legal).** The Privacy Policy lists the Practice fields, their purpose and retention before
    the flag is on; deleting a profile deletes every Practice row and seed log.
17. **Clamp.** Generator chance p\* in 0.02..0.98; every shown chance clamped to 2..98.

### 5.2 Core loop and session flow
One run = 10 rooms, about 3 minutes, one thumb.

| Time | Session 1 | Session 2 and later |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | First card appears; slider at 50; Lock | Same |
| 3–45 s | Rooms 1–3 (~14 s each: line appears, forecast, Lock, reveal) | Same |
| 45–50 s | — | **Door:** "Snaps back / Keeps going / Sudden jumps" sets rooms 4–6 |
| ~55 s | — | **Boss** (room 4): forecast, then a second forecast five steps later; the jump fires only after the second look |
| ~95 s | — | **Lens door:** Longer history (40 steps) / Weather label / Sister path (one ended path, same settings) for rooms 7–10 |
| 95–165 s | Rooms 4–9 | Rooms 7–9; **bosses** at 8 and 10 |
| 150–165 s | One announced **jump boss** at room 10 | Boss 10 |
| 165–180 s | Run summary | Run summary |

Roguelike elements: a fixed run of rooms; route choices between rooms (doors, lenses); content families; bosses; a run
summary; personal seeded runs; no retry (the permadeath analogue). Each lens must be the best one in some weather,
so choosing well means reading rooms 1–6, not memorising a winner.

### 5.3 Scoring maths
- Room points: `round(100 × (1 + log2 q))`, where q is the chance the player gave to what happened. 50 → 0 either way;
  55 → +14 / −15; 70 → +49 / −74; 90 → +85 / −232; 98 → +97 / −464.
- Expected for a forecast q on a room whose generator chance is p\*:
  `E(q) = 100 × (1 + p* log2 q + (1 − p*) log2 (1 − q))`. Best expected = `E(p*)`. Shortfall = Best − E(q) ≥ 0, zero only
  at q = p\* (a proper scoring rule: the honest forecast maximises expected points, and overconfidence costs on
  average even when lucky).
- Boss = mean of its two forecasts' points. Totals sum the rounded room points.
- **Worked example** (rooms with p\* 0.72, 0.55, 0.35 "higher"): Honest (says p\*) expects **+22**
  (14.5 + 0.7 + 6.6); Bold (90 on the favoured side) expects **−88** (−4.0, −57.8, −26.1); Fifty expects **0**. With
  outcomes higher, lower, lower: Honest +53 − 15 + 38 = **+76**, Bold 85 − 232 + 85 = **−62**. Had room 2 ended higher,
  Bold would score **+255** to Honest's +105, and the summary would still say "Expected for your forecasts: −88. Best
  expected here: +22." That is the design's lesson in one screen.
- **Owner-only note (never on a Practice page):** points equal the log growth of a paper split at even odds, so
  maximising expected points means forecasting honestly. This is how the owner's "make trades and try to maximize
  returns" survives: a game that paid average return would reward going all-in; this one pays log growth, which
  rewards only the honest forecast. Players see only: "Points reward saying how sure you are, honestly. 50 scores 0.
  Saying 90 when 70 is right costs you on average, even when you are lucky."

### 5.4 Progression
Week 1: even rooms. Week 2: doors and bosses. Week 3: lenses, and bucket bands on the Report at n ≥ 60. The finish cue
(5.1 rule 13) is followed by **Read the forecaster**: 20 forecasts by a generated bot, shown as a table with no handle,
avatar or chart, scored on whether the player can tell how calibrated the bot is. Then: "Nothing left to prove here."
Runs stay playable with expected lines only. No currency, unlocks for play count, streaks, badges or levels.

### 5.6 Sharing
"Copy route" copies text only: "Practice route: Snaps back, Keeps going, Weather label. Practice game. Generated
charts, not market data. No money." No numbers, seed, handle, link or image card, so a game result can never be
mistaken for, or posted as, a verified record.

### 5.7 Why it is engaging without compulsion
Players return because **each run asks a new question they can visibly get better at** (doors and lenses change the
weather; bosses add a second look; the Report shows shortfall falling), **the summary is honest** (it shows how much
of the run was luck), **and the game says when they have mastered it**. Players do **not** return because a lucky run
might come next: there is no outcome-based reward, no streak, no timer, no re-roll button, no currency, and after the
finish cue the luck line disappears. The variable part (outcome luck) is shown as noise, never paid out.

### 5.8 Metrics and stop rules
- **Learning:** median shortfall per room falls ≥ 30% from runs 1–3 to runs 8–10.
- **Finish reach:** 25–50% of players with 120 shown rooms reach the finish cue (tunes M).
- **Health:** verified-page views per Practice player not lower than holdout (upper 95% band of the change ≥ 0).
- **Compulsion guard:** < 5% of sessions contain more than 3 runs back to back.
- **First-session clarity:** 8 of 10 first-time players lock room 1 within 10 s; 4 of 5 explain "Expected" and "Luck
  moved this run" after one run; median room time ≤ 15 s; each door option chosen by 20–60% of runs.
- **Stop** if shortfall does not fall by week 8, the views band's upper bound is < 0, or on any compliance or trust and
  safety incident.

### 5.9 Timing gate: F-E (if the owner chooses it, O3)
F-A's session-1 run plus one door after room 5, behind a flag, for 28 days, with start and decision dates written
first. Eligible: signed-in, 18+ attested accounts whose first eligible visit is ≥ 7 days before the decision date;
holdout fixed by account hash. Primary: D7 return to a verified page (days 1–7). Band: unpooled two-proportion 95%.
Power: +5 points on a 15% base needs about 900 per arm if the whole arm plays, about 20,500 per arm at 20% uptake, so
at week-8 scale the expected result is "stop"; **the owner signs before the start that a stop means "build after the
gates", not "the idea failed"**. Stop if the lift's lower band ≤ 0, if the upper band of the change in verified profile
views per viewer is < 0, or on any incident. Practice rows are deleted 30 days after the stop.

### 5.10 Risks
Players may read only "This run" before the finish cue (mitigated by rule 11's order); lens posteriors need discrete
generator families; a mis-tuned M leaves only luck varying (playtest it); the game may pull attention from verified
records (the health metric and stop rule); points could be read as "performance" (O1); a generated line can still be
mistaken for market data (strip, data rule, no tickers).

See `docs/features/F-8-prediction-game.md` → Acceptance criteria (AC-1 to AC-24). They are the round-5 criteria with
each committee demand turned into a check.

## 6. Alternate 1: F-D · Practice, staged
Day one is exactly F-A. A **Range run** row (F-B's content) is built only if F-A players' 28-day return to Practice is
≥ 25% and F-A's stop rules have not fired, and appears after the first Forecast finish cue or 14 days after the first
run. One card shell (same header, Exit and Lock, generator, seed service, Report); the two scores are never summed.
Adds the **Twin room** (one room per run asks for a range, then a side, on the same line, then shows "Your range and
your forecast differ by 22"). Strength: two different judgements, each with an end. Objections (3): the 14-day unlock
reads as a return hook (responsible design asks for the finish cue only); complexity. Choose it if the owner wants a
path beyond one mode. Full text: `dialectic/r5-synthesis.md` → F-D.

## 7. Alternate 2: F-B · Range Run
Ten rooms of "Where does this line end?" answered as a low and high percent with presets (Narrow, Medium, Wide) and
steppers. Card = `100 − 15 × (width/s + 10 × miss/s)` (s = 5): a percent outside costs ten times a percent of width, so
the best range is the honest 80% range. Worked: Honest −6..+6 expects 50.7 a room, Bold −2..+2 25.9, Wide −12..+12
27.5. Bias bosses (a decoy line, a recent spike, a round number) target the player's own lean and retire when it is
gone; the run closes by naming its weather (four-option log4 score, never added to cards). Strength: teaches how wide
uncertainty really is. Objections (13): the heaviest input at 360 px, four statistic modules, and its plank-walk
animation breaks the 400 ms motion rule (it would become a static reveal). Full text: `dialectic/r5-synthesis.md` → F-B.

**Recorded, not recommended: F-C · This Week's Five.** Five shared generated paths a week, one range question each,
truth and crowd density after an absolute close time, seed committed by hash at open. Honestly the "other game-like
feature", not a roguelike. It needs ≥ 20 distinct weekly players for 4 weeks before it can even open, and a weekly
close is a soft deadline; 15 of 20 objected.

## 9. Known limits (ponytail)
Each limit is followed by what would remove it.
- **9,000 of the 10,000 variants are machine recombinations** of 1,000 agent-written seeds, not independent ideas.
  Removal: more seed generators with different angles, or human-written seeds.
- **Stage 2a scored by formula** (per-option weights and text flags), so a strong idea with unlucky enum values could
  be cut before any panel read it. Removal: LLM read-through of all 10,000 in batches, or a random audit sample of the
  cut 9,500 (none was run).
- **Simulated panels, judges and committee.** Every score and vote came from AI agents voicing roles; no human or
  real player judged anything. Removal: the playtests in 5.8 and the owner's review.
- **Per-panel z-scores** assume the four 2b batches were equally strong (they were dealt round-robin from a sorted
  list, which helps). Removal: overlap items scored by two panels.
- **Engagement is untested.** "Players return because…" is a hypothesis; no retention figure in this record is
  measured. Removal: F-E or a playtest cohort.
- **Finish margin M (5 points per room) and the 60-room floor** are reasoned, not calibrated. Removal: playtest.
- **Generator realism.** Whether generated lines feel like markets enough to be interesting, and are clearly not
  market data, is untested. Removal: a 10-player comparison of generated vs masked historical lines (masked history
  also needs a data licence and re-identification checks).
- **Legal points are AI findings** (owner decision 2026-11-01), including the F6 "shape of a wager" ruling.
- **Builders must mark any limit they leave in code with a `ponytail:` comment** (AC-24).
