# F-8 dialectic: common brief (every role reads this)

Product: "Stock Social": public, broker-verified trading records (percent returns only), a vertical swipe feed
and public profiles; adults 18+ only. The owner asked for a game-like layer rewarding good predictions, e.g. a
~3-minute roguelike session of paper trades and price predictions, no real money, design only. The goal is "most
engaging and replayable for adults, within POLICY": engagement must come from mastery, curiosity, social
comparison and craft, never from compulsion.

Binding limits (docs/POLICY.md F1, F3a, F3b, F5, F6, P3, P4, A1 and CLAUDE.md product rules; read the files at
~/stock-social): no real money; no points or prizes convertible to money; no prizes at all; no loot boxes or
variable-ratio rewards; no rewards for real-trade frequency; no confetti on real trades; no placing trades; no
advice, signals or "buy X" prompts, and no crowd consensus on a named live security; nothing pushed whose content
or timing comes from price moves (F3b); adults only; game results never mixed into, shown beside as equivalent to,
or labelled like a verified record; WCAG 2.2 AA. Prefer calibrated judgement (proper scoring rules that punish
overconfidence) over luck. Docs must not name any third-party company, app, game or real person (POLICY T1/T2):
describe genres generically.

Files: the shortlist for the round is named in your task. Write only the file your task names, in markdown,
with one "### <design id> · <name>" section per design (same ids as the shortlist; a synthesis may create merged
ids like "S2-03"). Keep each design section under ~350 words. Reply with <= 5 lines.
