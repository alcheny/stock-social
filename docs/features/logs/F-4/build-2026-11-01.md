# F-4 · Rework build of 2026-11-01 (with F-6's share-card part)

Plan and decisions: `committee-2026-11-01.md` (this folder). This file records the build, the QA and user tests, what
each round changed, and the test results.

## What was built

- **Statistics.**
  - `lib/bets.ts` (new, pure leaf): `betClusters` (exactly `confidence()`'s bets) and `arithmeticRange`. The range is
    the 95% range around the arithmetic average per trade: a cluster-robust SE with t at K − 1 df, and its low end
    stopped at −100%.
  - `lib/confidence.ts`:
    - `tierFor` with the thresholds named once (`STRONG`, `MODERATE`);
    - the 2026-10-31 trust table, with the under-5 row and the same words for gains and losses;
    - `trustSign` from the printed interval alone;
    - `spanBetween` rounding days as the profile's evidence line does;
    - the ranking floor comment updated to the owner's decision.
- **The card model (`lib/record-card.ts`).**
  - `cardEvidence`: range, tier and verdict, shared with the profile.
  - The running-sum series (`cardSeries`): it starts at the first entry, ends at count × average, and keeps an
    `'account'` seam for F-5 that refuses to draw.
  - The period runs from the first trade to the last close, in one unit per tier.
  - `cardOpen` (F-6): count, up and down, lowest and highest. No symbols, no average, no combined figure.
  - `cardDescription`: the preview text built from the card.
  - `cardLink` accepts an origin with no scheme.
  - The headline label reads "average per closed trade" when there are open positions.
- **The image (`record-card-image.tsx`).**
  - The caption has its own row on the link preview, and the open-positions row is added at every size.
  - The headline never shrinks under its label.
  - The @handle always shows in full.
  - If the content ever overflows, only the middle can be clipped; the trust line and disclaimer never are.
- **Data (`record-card-data.ts`).** It reads `unrealized_pct` only. `recordCard` shows open positions only for a
  broker record with no import running.
- **Profile (`app/u/[handle]/page.tsx`, additive, F-4 block).**
  - The preview text is the card's own words plus the disclaimer, with no win rate.
  - The range, tier and "Past results" verdict come from `cardEvidence`, so the profile and the card it was shared
    from agree.
  - The share prompt quotes the card's range.
- **Copy (`lib/copy.ts`, F-4 block).**
  - New strings: the caption, "average per closed trade" and the open-positions words.
  - The share prompt's "what's public" sentence now names open positions.

## Rounds

1. **QA and user tests of the first build.**
   - Tester: not clean. One blocking defect (the headline overlapped its label), two major (a range below −100%; the
     card's tier differed from the profile's) and four minor.
   - Business lead: FAIL on compliance and business. The preview text had no disclaimer; the handle could disappear;
     the profile and card disagreed; the headline overlapped; spacing; the disclaimer colour.
   - UX lead: FAIL on usability. The headline gap; profile/card ranges; chart clearance; one-position wording; minus
     signs; the size floor.
   - Code review: no blocking finding. Should-fix: the profile and card could show different tiers or verdicts; nits
     on query gating, the alt wording, the printed sign and source-grep tests.
2. **The fix round.** Everything above was fixed except the items below, each with its reason.
   - The UX lead's minus sign: `pct()` prints an ASCII hyphen across the whole product, and the card follows it.
   - The 16 px space before the count became 10 px, and it drops in the worst case so the open-positions row is never
     clipped.
   - The profile chart still compounds: it is the profile's chart, not the card's (hand-off).
   - Zero-width ranges ("likely between +2.0% and +2.0%" when every return is equal) are mathematically right and left
     as is.
   - A span under a day reads "1 day", not "under 1 day" (committee decision 6).
   - Code review #4 (an extra query per profile view, acceptable now), #7 (old tests read `chart.down`) and #8 (the F-5
     seam, decided) were not changed.

3. **Round 2.**
   - Business lead: compliance PASS, business PASS. Two items were recorded rather than built: the profile chart is
     an open question in the feature file, and thousands separators in percentages ("+1007.8%") are a follow-up for
     `pct()`, which is shared by every surface.
   - UX lead: FAIL on one blocking defect. In open_tight's square and story the "unrealized, not sold yet" note was
     clipped under the evidence line. Fixed: the note moved onto the open-positions title row (it wraps only if the
     row is full), and "at 0.0%" lost its indent. Re-rendered: every row is visible in open_tight at all three sizes.
   - Code review: no blocking finding. Fixed: a trader with no display name shows the handle at name size, and it
     never truncates; "too wide" reads the unclamped half-width. Left as a hand-off (open question in the feature
     file): the trade, month and compare pages still print `confidence()`'s range.

4. **After F-6 merged (2026-11-01).**
   - The card counts and tones open positions with F-6's `lib/open-split.ts` (`openSummary`, `tone`). The stand-in
     `cardOpen` keeps only the card's own wording (lowest/highest).
   - The text under the link preview now follows F-6's one wording for open positions in text: "3 open positions ·
     2 up, 1 down · +2.5% avg unrealized", the same on the profile and the feed, after the card's closed-trade figures.
     A record with no closes keeps its empty-record line (F-6, PR-2b), so the earlier change to `e2e/connect.spec.ts`
     is reverted and that file matches main again.
   - The picture keeps "lowest, highest" (committee Q-U). Both describe the same positions and never contradict.

5. **Orchestrator review (2026-11-01).** Fixed:
   - A wrapped range or a long average could clip the open-positions row on the link preview. Now the text column
     widens and the chart narrows (`unfurlWide`), an average of 8 or more characters is drawn smaller
     (`unfurlHeadlineSize`), and the caption and open-positions row never shrink. New e2e states: `wide_open`,
     `wide_failed`, `huge_open`.
   - The F-2 screenshots that the full e2e run overwrote are restored from main.
   - The copy and the decision now agree ("3 open, unrealized").
   - A record under half a day reads "1 day" on the card and the profile alike.
   - `cardOpen` reuses `lib/open-split.ts`.
   - The display log (privacy #31) records the profile's new range and evidence (`cardEvidence`, `evidenceRangeText`).
