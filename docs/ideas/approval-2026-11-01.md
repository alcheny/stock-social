and requirements (POLICY H; owner decision 2026-11-01) and asked to increase the rate of development. Fifteen members

## Process

- **Round 0 (consistency check).** Council proposals had not been through the idea board, so the decisions keeper,
  common sense and legal checked every candidate first. One candidate went to the owner (C16), the rest were
  reshaped, and nothing was rejected outright. Their full verdicts are in Appendix A.
- **Round 1 (pitch and first vote).** The chair wrote 21 briefs (C1-C21; C22 folded into C12). Candidates came from
  the approval-council items in `docs/IDEAS.md` (D20-D29, ideas 13 and 14), the creative direction council
  (CR-1..CR-37, #34), the design direction council (P-1..P-10, OD-1..OD-14, #38) and the market research council
  (MR-1..MR-14, read from its open branch). After round 0 the ballot held 20 items. All 15 members scored each one 0-5.
- **Round 3 (debate).** The five highest and five lowest scorers on each candidate stated their strongest argument,
  and every member could change any score for a stated reason. The chair recorded facts raised in round 1: paper
  accounts are already excluded through the vendor flag (C20), and the fixes list covers parts of C1, C5, C7, C10,
  C11 and C13. The chair ruled that a feature is what remains after its fixes.
- **Round 4 (second vote).** The top half after the debate (10 items) went to a second vote. The other 10 dropped to
  the next cycle.
- **Round 5 (red team and final vote).** Eight items met the 3.5 mean: C8, C3, C7, C15, C6, C19, C1 and C9. The red
  team wrote the case against each, and all 15 members then voted approve, approve with conditions, or reject.

Member key: BL business lead, MR market research lead, PF pricing and finance, GR growth, UX UX lead, PD product
designer, DV data-visualization designer, A11Y accessibility specialist, EN engineer, RE reliability engineer, LG legal
and compliance, TS trust and safety, DK decisions keeper (veto), CS common sense (veto), RT red team.

<details><summary>r5-red-team-case</summary>

#### Red team: the case against each shortlisted item (round 5)

##### C8 Thesis safety v2, report, review queue
Failure: NFKC plus a confusables map holds ordinary theses. "Sold at 2x ATR", "see chart in bio" and non-Latin names trip the link, contact or look-alike rules. Abuse: a few accounts mass-report a rival's honest thesis, auto-hold buries it, and with the queue off it can never be released. Held becomes dropped in practice, which breaks U1. Cost: M-L, the biggest build on the slate. Neutralise: a negative corpus with 0 false holds; auto-hold needs N distinct reporters with signed-in accounts older than X days; the author sees the held state and why; a release path (owner-run script) exists before the queue does.

##### C3 One record line on every feed card
Failure: "12 closed · avg +4.1% (likely −2% to +10%)" on a card still reads as skill to a casual scroller, and the strip lends a lucky trader extra authority. Cost: per-card aggregates add N+1 queries on the hottest route, and three ticker PRs triple the review load while racing #30's rewrite. Card height breaks swipe at 360x640. Neutralise: one aggregate query with a load test; built after #30; reflow e2e at 320 px and landscape; "Too early to tell" under 5 bets; short-record wording from the design system.

##### C7 Record status line
Failure: "updated 2 hours ago" gets cached in ISR, the CDN or an OG image, and a record disconnected for a month still looks fresh. That is a false trust claim, worse than none. "Updated" taken from the cron schedule instead of the last successful sync lies after silent sync failures. Cost: S-M across five surfaces. Neutralise: computed at request time from last_synced_at only; absolute dates on images; the TR-5 states covered by e2e; shipped with or before C19.

##### C15 Record strip on trade and month surfaces and images
Failure: platforms crop share images to a square or a 1.91:1 thumbnail. The strip and disclaimer are cut, and the lone big green number circulates as an ad. Duplicating C3 builds two strips that drift apart in wording. Cost: M on image routes beside #35, with merge conflicts likely. Neutralise: one RecordStrip with C3, after #35; an anti-crop band tested at the smallest platform crop; alt text carries the facts; no win rate on images.

##### C6 First-visit explanation and swipe hint
Failure: "Verified trades, straight from the broker. Losses included." sits above a feed that is 100% demo, and a screenshot of it misrepresents the product as live. With storage blocked the line reappears on every visit. Its stated metric (bounce before and after) can't be read because C17 was cut. Cost: S, but an F3a exposure. Neutralise: the Demo badge in the same viewport (an e2e test); renders and dismisses with storage blocked; no before/after claim.

##### C19 Sync cadence within vendor guidance
Failure: the throttle also throttles /connect/done, so a newly connected trader sees an empty record for up to 24 h and abandons. Or a clock or column bug blocks every sync and records freeze silently. Cost: S, but it depends on an outside session's schedule and slips if that session does. Neutralise: a test that the first sync is never throttled; a test counting vendor calls per account-day; ships only with C7's freshness line.

##### C1 Disclaimer in plain view
Failure: it becomes a test-only PR that takes a whole feature slot from a cap of six while fix #3 does the real work. A hand-kept route list goes stale, so the next performance page ships without the disclaimer. Fixed bars cover it at 360 px. Cost: S. Neutralise: generate the route list from the route tree; fold it into fix #3's PR or C3's session rather than giving it a slot.

##### C9 Handle and name integrity
Failure: skeleton look-alike matching blocks real handles ("supp0rt_trader", names close to demo handles), and the rejection gives no reason, so signups abandon. A blunt list ships with third-party names and fails T1. Cost: M if look-alike rules spread into the rename flow. Neutralise: look-alikes checked only against reserved, demo and connected handles; the message names the rule broken; no third-party names (scanner); 301s cut.
